Homeroom

A mockup of a website where each class gets its own room. The teacher shares one link, students find the weekly reading and hand in homework there, and the teacher watches or reads it right in the browser.

1
Teacher: your classesOne card per class, with its room link to copy.
2
Teacher: review homeworkOpen a class room, switch weeks, watch or read what students handed in.
3
Student: the roomWhat a student sees after opening the link. Works on a phone, an iPad or a computer.
This is a mockup, not the real thing yet. Nothing is saved, uploaded or downloaded, and the names and files are made up. Things worth trying:
  • On the student page, tap the week buttons at the top.
  • On the teacher room page, click different students and press "Mark as reviewed".
  • Tell Ricky what feels right, what feels wrong, and what is missing.
